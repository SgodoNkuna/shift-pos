# Steps review: taps for every everyday task

Counted on the live app (one-item examples). "Typing" = entering numbers or words.

| Task | Before | Now | What changed |
|---|---|---|---|
| Open the app and sign in | 4 (PIN) | 4 | Own phone: only your name, so just the PIN |
| Clock in | 1 | 1 | Opening stock comes from the last shift |
| Clock in with a wrong count | not possible | 2 + typing | "Correct a count", flagged on the report |
| Cash sale, exact money | 3 | 3 | Item → **Cash** → Record (exact is pre-chosen) |
| Cash sale with change | 4 | 4 | Item → Cash → R100 → Record |
| Card sale | 4 | **3** | Item → **Card** → Record (was Pay → Card → Record) |
| Part cash, part card | 5 | 5 | Item → "Part cash, part card" → amount → Record |
| Undo the last sale | 1 | 1 | "Undo" next to "Last sale" |
| Stock in (24 Coke) | 5 | 5 | In → product → +12 → +12 → Add |
| Stock out | 4 | 4 | Out → product → Send (reason pre-chosen) |
| Boss/manager approves | 2 | 2 | Tap the alert → Approve |
| Expense | 3 + typing | 3 + typing | Expense → amount → note → Save |
| Count at clock out (24 items) | 24 | **3** + only the differences | "All the rest match" per group |
| Card slip + cash count | typing | typing | Two boxes |
| Handover PINs | 2 + 8 digits | 2 + 8 digits | Both people's PINs |
| Lock and send the report | 2 + share | **1** + share | "Lock & send report" opens WhatsApp sharing |

Kept on purpose: the paper-sheet flow (open → sell → in/out → count → lock → send), both PINs at handover,
approval for every stock out, and a count for every item (one tap per group when nothing differs).
